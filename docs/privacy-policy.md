# Bhumitra Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 28 September 2026_

Bhumitra ("we", "us") helps you find land parcels in Odisha and view their official land records. This policy explains what information the Bhumitra iOS app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it.

## Information we collect

| Information | Why we use it | Linked to you? |
|---|---|---|
| **Name and email address** — from Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In | To create and secure your account | Yes |
| **Account ID** | To keep your credits, purchases and saved plots attached to your account | Yes |
| **Device identifier** (generated by the app) | To grant free starter searches once per device and prevent abuse | Yes |
| **Purchase history** — plot-search credit packs and subscriptions | To apply what you bought to your account and restore purchases | Yes |
| **Precise location** — only while the app is open, and only if you allow it | To show where you are on the cadastral map. It is used on your device and is not stored on our servers | No |
| **App usage events** (e.g. screens opened, searches run) | To understand which features work and improve the app | No |
| **Crash and performance data** | To find and fix bugs | No |

**Land records you look up** (plot number, khata, owner names shown in the official Record of Rights) come from public Government of Odisha sources such as Bhulekh and IGR Odisha. We fetch them on your request and may cache them to make repeat lookups faster. We do not sell this data.

We do **not** track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do **not** show third-party advertising.

## Payments

All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your card or bank details. We receive a transaction record from Apple so we can add credits or activate your subscription.

## Third-party services

We use these providers to run the app. Each processes data only to provide its service to us:

- **Apple** — Sign in with Apple, App Store payments.
- **Google** — Google Sign-In (if you choose it); **Firebase Analytics** (usage events) and **Firebase Crashlytics** (crash reports).
- **Esri (ArcGIS)** — satellite and street map tiles. Esri receives the map area being viewed, not your account details.
- **Government of Odisha portals** (Bhulekh, IGR Odisha) — the source of official land records and benchmark values.
- **Cloud hosting** — our backend servers that store your account, credits and purchases.

## How long we keep data

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. Analytics and crash data is kept by Firebase according to its retention settings (up to 14 months). When you delete your account, your personal data is deleted as described below.

## Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account inside the app: **Profile → Manage Account → Delete account**. This deletes your account, sign-in identities, credits, usage history and personal data from our servers.

We keep a record that your device has already received its free starter searches (without your name or email), so deleting and recreating an account does not grant new free searches. If you have an active subscription, cancel it in **iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions**; deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

You can also ask us to delete your data by emailing **bhumitra74@gmail.com**.

## Your choices

- You can refuse or turn off location access in iPhone Settings; the map still works without it.
- You can sign out at any time.
- You can ask us for a copy of, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at **bhumitra74@gmail.com**.

## Children

Bhumitra is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

## Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
